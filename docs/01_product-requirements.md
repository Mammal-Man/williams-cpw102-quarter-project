# Product Requirements Document


## Problem

Games rely too much on UI and fancy graphics

## Desired outcome

Create a game that plays entirely in the terminal

## Scope

[Which users, use cases, and features will this app cover?]

Support multiple difficulty modes and prebuilt challenges.  
Dialogue - influence the story through choices  
Combat - Fight opponents with elements of RNG and strategy



## Out of Scope

[What features is this app specifically avoiding?]
Save files - no data related to the game is saved  
UI - The game will be played using just the command terminal, no mouse required

## Requirements


### The product must

- Be able to interpret a users inputs to move the story
- Communicate to the player the rules of the game
- Track player stats and story progression

### The product could

- Support optional challenges or difficulty modes by limiting/manipulating mechanics available to the player or increasing enemy power by modifying stats or adding new abilities
- Have randomly generated rooms with different amounts/types of enemies
